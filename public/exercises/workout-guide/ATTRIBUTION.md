# Basse exercise artwork attribution

Basse redistributes selected artwork from `@bryllim/workout-guide@1.0.0`.

Artwork license: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

The PNG files are copied byte-for-byte. Basse applies grayscale, contrast and brightness styling at runtime. Any Basse-specific adaptation is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

The complete upstream notice is retained in `UPSTREAM-ATTRIBUTION.md`. Per-file records and hashes are retained in `ASSET-MANIFEST.json` and `ASSETS.sha256`.
