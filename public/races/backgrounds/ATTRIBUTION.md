## Production delivery policy

Race-background source files are delivered in production as optimized WebP derivatives.

- Production filenames retain the verified source stem and use the `.webp` extension.
- Standard derivatives use automatic EXIF orientation, a maximum dimension of 2560 px, and WebP quality 82.
- `livigno.webp` uses WebP quality 77 to remain below the 1.2 MB delivery limit.
- Metadata is stripped during production encoding.
- Resizing, orientation normalization, format conversion, and documented mobile cropping do not imply photographer endorsement.
- Each image remains governed by the source license stated in its attribution record.
- CC BY and CC BY-SA derivatives retain the attribution, license link, and modification notice required by their respective licenses.
- `DERIVATIVES.sha256` records the exact production files.
